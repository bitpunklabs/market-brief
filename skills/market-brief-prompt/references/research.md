# Shared research capabilities

These specify outcomes, combined with a market profile and selected cadence.

## Evidence and expectation changes

The receiving research task includes web search and source verification, with
the access-limitation fallback described under delivery quality.
Establish a broad view before selecting material developments. Search supporting
evidence, deterioration, failed responses, clarifications, delays, and competing
explanations. Prioritize original releases, regulatory records, issuer disclosures,
and reproducible data; reporting supplies leads and context. Consolidate duplicate
coverage by first-public event. Separate facts, analytical inferences, and forward
hypotheses. Compare results with sourced prior expectations, previous readings,
revisions, or prior guidance; label the comparator accurately.
Describe whether a development creates a new mechanism, reinforces an existing
thesis, delivers a priced expectation, or damages the earlier logic.

Trace new fact → expectation change → operating/financing/valuation/risk mechanism
→ observed pricing → unresolved question. Distinguish initial catalyst, amplifying
mechanism, and persistent effect. Support leading attributions with chronology,
alternative explanations, counterevidence, and evidence-calibrated confidence.

## Time and measurement

Resolve actual time and market calendar at execution. State review interval,
market-data endpoint, news cutoff, and forward horizon. Separate occurrence,
first disclosure, update, and data reference dates. Historical research uses
information and data vintages public by its cutoff. Post-endpoint information
updates the outlook separately; earlier attribution uses information then available.
Historical tasks retain that information boundary throughout the report. Add later
updates only on a separately requested update, stating its independent cutoff.
For a forward period already begun, distinguish outcomes from its future remainder.

Use consistent instruments, samples, currencies, return definitions, and endpoints.
Calculate returns as end/start − 1 or compound subperiod returns. Separate price
returns, total returns, yields, and spread changes; rate/spread changes use basis
points. Describe eligibility, weighting, adjustments, and missing history for
aggregates. Exact attribution requires comparable constituents, weights and inputs;
otherwise provide bounded qualitative reasoning. Normalize activity flows for
period length while preserving the meaning of stock measures.

## Market pricing and transmission

Compare benchmarks with broad participation, concentration, sectors/styles, and
matched-window relative returns. Explain decisive event windows and response
persistence. Distinguish absolute gains from relative resilience. Trace demand,
costs, margins, financing, discount rates, currency, competition, and risk premia.
Price, turnover and participation evaluate how expectations are priced. Cross-market
evidence states timestamps, session status, currency and release lags; label stale
observations. Multiple asset responses to one event share one underlying cause.

## Operating fundamentals, sectors, and themes

Scan economically relevant areas broadly, including weak and unchanged ones; expand
material issues. Separate industries from overlapping themes. Follow demand, supply,
orders, pricing, inventory, competition, capacity, margins, cash flow, investment
and policy implementation as appropriate. Explain actual exposure of representatives:
beneficiaries, suppliers, customers, cost bearers, and competitors. Assess operating
trend, priced expectations, and upcoming catalysts separately; preserve thesis
horizons across editions. Valuation states economic claim, metric, denominator,
forecast horizon and historical sample. Separate realized improvement, forecast
revisions, and forecast windows rolling forward. Monthly research deepens valuation;
shorter cadences use it where a material change warrants discussion.

## Outlook, events, risk, and continuity

Develop a substantive forward view of the market or sectors/themes/entities from
the consequential unresolved questions established earlier. Explain the better-supported
judgment, mechanism, and gap between economic delivery and priced expectations.
Integrate assumptions, competing explanations, counterevidence and reasons to revise
naturally. Identify concrete disclosures, operating outcomes or implementation
milestones, why they matter and when evidence can arrive. An unchanged view retains
its logic/horizon with updates to evidence and unresolved links.

Events supply verified dates, time zones, certainty, sourced expectations or prior
state, and the question tested. Major risks specify trigger, transmission, affected
exposures and contrary evidence. Retain significant judgments with original evidence
and horizons. When a real prior report exists, compare post-publication outcomes
against original terms. Evaluate direction, mechanism, timing and risk separately:
supported, partially supported, invalidated, not triggered, still awaiting validation.
Leave untriggered conditions and unfinished horizons open. Without a prior report,
complete review and outlook using current evidence. Research priorities guide
further investigation and observation.

## Delivery quality

Use retrieved facts or reproducible calculations; match precision to evidence.
Disclose material gaps near affected judgments, reduce confidence accordingly and
complete verifiable work. Without browsing, state the limitation and identify the
output's non-current scope. Execution selects a disclosed reference universe from
available sources; user watchlists add context.

Deliver one integrated report, conclusions first, in the resolved language. State
period/cutoffs and meaningful limitations near the title. Use a # title, ## main
sections, natural paragraphs, compact comparison tables, and short observation or
calendar lists. Keep cells brief, one record per row, blank lines around tables,
and dense sources in adjacent prose. Cite facts, numbers and evidence supporting
judgments near claims through supported citations or readable links. Explain each
fact once, developing implications later. Prioritize substantive completeness
within the cadence's approximate length.
Conclude with the important judgment or change, concrete evidence needed next,
and the principal reason to revise, keeping these consistent with the outlook.
