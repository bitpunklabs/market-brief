# US equities profile

## Institutions and evidence

Use America/New_York with Asia/Shanghai secondary event times. Verify exchange
calendars, daylight saving, regular/extended sessions and early closes. Benchmarks
include S&P 500, Nasdaq-100, Russell 2000 and relevant sectors/styles; compare
cap/equal weight, large/small, growth/value and participation. Specify samples
and corporate-action adjustments.

Prioritize Fed, BLS, BEA, Treasury, other original releasing bodies, SEC filings,
issuer IR, earnings releases and conference calls. Cite consensus, previous
guidance/readings, revisions and release times. Earnings comparisons align fiscal
quarter, currency, GAAP/non-GAAP, forecast horizon and sample weighting.

## Fundamental transmission and earnings

Separate growth, inflation, labor/consumption, policy path, term premium, financing
and risk appetite. Investigate why yields change before interpreting equity effects.
Distinguish policy actions, communication and market-implied expectations with
source/time. Use nominal/real yields, curve, dollar, direct credit spreads, volatility
and commodities where material. Distinguish spot VIX, futures volatility, bond-fund
prices and yields. Global evidence matches windows and explains demand/currency/
cost/competition transmission.

Earnings are an independent responsibility. Consequential issuers need revenue/EPS
versus proper expectations, guidance, segments/orders/demand, margins/cash flow,
capex and financing. Separate results/outlook, operating/one-off changes,
authorization/execution, press release/completed call. Trace effects on issuer,
suppliers, customers, competitors and indices. Incomplete after-hours disclosures
remain preliminary. Earnings-season samples state coverage/cutoff/weighting and
distinguish isolated, industry and broad improvement.

## Pricing, valuation, and flows

Scan major GICS sectors before subsectors/overlapping themes. Explain actual
revenue/cost/capex exposure. Separate relative resilience from absolute gains and
heavyweights from broad stocks. Exact contributions need weights, matched returns
and method. Postmarket separates previous close→open gap and open→close return:
(1+gap)×(1+intraday)−1. Distinguish trigger, amplification and durable repricing.

Monthly earnings separates realized growth, forecast revisions, rolling NTM changes,
guidance and EPS effects of buybacks/tax/one-offs. Valuation is distinct: metric,
earnings basis/horizon, historical sample, discount rate and required delivery.
Earnings/multiple diagnostics use compatible inputs and separate dividends.
Treat decomposition as an arithmetic diagnostic; substantiate its causal
interpretation with economic evidence. Compare monthly macro/policy expectations
at the beginning and end on consistent definitions and dated observations.
Macro depth includes job quality/wages/hours, consumption/income/savings/credit,
inflation components, investment/housing, fiscal changes, seasonality and revisions.
Distinguish year-over-year/sequential and adjusted/unadjusted measures, with base
effects, reference periods and revision vintages stated.
Simple yield comparisons are limited risk-premium diagnostics.

Monthly earnings-cycle and valuation analysis are separately visible priorities.
Each develops its own economic judgment and unresolved question before synthesis.
Use the same forecast year or compatible horizons to assess revisions. Weekly
sector comparisons identify persistent earnings support, pending delivery and
reasons to change research priority.

Subscriptions, trading, AUM revaluation, holdings and institutional disclosures
retain separate meanings/dates. Verify reference/release/transaction dates and lags
for 13F/CFTC/options/flows. Position direction and dealer/CTA/short-covering
attribution require evidence; explain volume/open-interest limits. Expiry/rebalancing/
passive effects need observed support. Assess executed buybacks, financing/dilution,
liquidity, credit and correlated concentration. Central-bank/Treasury aggregates
inform a transmission argument with explicit assumptions.
