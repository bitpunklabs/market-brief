# Crypto profile

## Institutions, time, and evidence

Continuous trading; UTC research boundaries with Asia/Shanghai secondary times.
Verify venue/instrument/fund/derivatives/banking/settlement calendars individually.
Reference BTC, ETH, ETH/BTC and a disclosed liquid risk-token universe. Prioritize
official macro/policy/court records, fund filings, exchange data/specifications,
protocol/governance/status documents and verifiable transactions. Aggregation
uses methodology-disclosed providers and stated coverage.

## Data meaning and eligible comparisons

- Identify provider/venue/instrument, currency, timestamp, units and aggregation.
  Separate spot/index/mark/perpetual/dated-futures/fund observations; USD/USDT/USDC
  need depeg context. Price return, staking income, funding P&L and airdrops are
  distinct. Boundary returns use matched prices; period volume uses disjoint
  measurements, rolling snapshots retain actual overlapping windows.
- Risk breadth uses unique liquid tokens; stablecoins, wrapped/bridged duplicates,
  leveraged tokens and tokenized external assets are analyzed separately. State
  sample/eligibility/weighting, migrations/new listings/missing histories and
  historical constituent quality. Use point-in-time constituents where available;
  label survivor-biased or illustrative samples when only later/current rosters
  are available. Define dominance/ex-BTC/ex-ETH denominators and stablecoin
  inclusion. Market cap reflects price/supply/constituents; returns
  and flows need independent measurements. Themes retain explicit overlap.
- Separate spot demand, product subscriptions, AUM revaluation, fund turnover,
  executed treasury purchases, stablecoin issuance/circulation, exchange/bridge
  transfers. State periods, coverage, release lags and provisional/inferred status.
  Transfers establish movement; purchases/sales require execution evidence. Trace
  minted stablecoins to actual deployment. Combine only compatible non-overlapping
  flows. Missing/delayed data remain unknown.
  State uncertainty and vintage of wallet/entity labels used for attribution.
- Open interest distinguishes contracts/coins, USD notional, price revaluation and
  provider one-/two-sided counting. Verify settled/predicted funding, settlement
  interval and normalization. Separate perpetual funding, dated basis, options
  premium/notional, tenor and volatility/skew convention. Liquidation notional
  measures contracts closed; cash losses need separate evidence. Depth specifies
  venue/price band/time. Hidden positions and directional attribution need evidence.
  Longer-period research distinguishes leverage entering the period, changes during
  key events and the configuration remaining at its endpoint.
- Separate circulating/total/max supply, float, issuance/burns, vesting/unlocks,
  recipient availability, distribution, exchange transfers and selling. Verify
  units/recipients/restrictions/schedule and emissions overlap. Assess supply
  pressure against float, executable liquidity and measured demand.

## Economic mechanisms and transmission

Trace adoption/demand → fees/surplus → actual recipient → active holder benefit →
incentives/issuance/dilution → retained value/risk. Separate network/app success,
operating-company income and token-holder economics. Distinguish proposal/approval/
activation/execution. Monetary/collateral/settlement/security/utility assets use
appropriate evidence for their economic role.
Identify revenue-funded versus treasury-funded distributions, burns, buybacks or
support, their recipients and actual execution.

Assess fee-paying activity, retention, share, fees/revenue, incentive dependence and
methodology changes. TVL measures a stock; addresses and transfers retain their
observed meaning, with users/payments requiring inference evidence. Compare price-
adjusted metrics and disjoint activity; monthly flows use daily averages too.
Valuation states numerator/denominator, claim, supply basis, horizon and sample.
FDV/circulating value answer different supply questions. Sustainable holder benefits
and dilution guide valuation; temporary fee spikes retain their measured duration.

Scan monetary assets, settlement/scaling/data availability, DeFi/trading,
stablecoin/payment/RWA infrastructure, staking/security, oracle/interoperability,
compute/storage/AI/DePIN, consumer/gaming/NFT and speculative assets. Select material
themes with actual token exposure, including weak/unchanged areas. Separate
beneficiaries, suppliers, users/cost bearers, competitors and tokenless activities.
Daily depth follows changes; monthly centers adoption, holder economics and supply.

Cross-assets test growth/inflation/policy/liquidity/risk mechanisms with matched
live/stale windows. Monthly analysis compares beginning/end macro and policy
expectations with dated sources, distinguishing actions, communication and implied
pricing. Separate crypto-specific/shared drivers. Regulation/product
access states jurisdiction and filing→approval→effective rule→launch→actual demand.
Security distinguishes allegation/exploit/loss/recovery/residual exposure. Assess
stablecoin redemption, custody/exchange/collateral concentration, bridge/oracle,
governance and financing risks, stating reserve/liability/solvency evidence scope.
Correlated dependencies inform exposure. Event times distinguish confirmed clocks,
block/epoch estimates, votes/execution and conditional milestones.
