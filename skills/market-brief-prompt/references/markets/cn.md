# CN equities profile

## Institutions and evidence

Use Asia/Shanghai and official exchange calendars. Verify current rules of
Shanghai, Shenzhen, Beijing exchanges and relevant boards/products at execution.
State universe eligibility for ST, new listings and suspensions. Reference broad
and size/style benchmarks such as Shanghai Composite, CSI 300, CSI 500/1000,
ChiNext and STAR according to task and data availability.

Prioritize government releases, NBS, PBOC, Ministry of Finance, regulators,
exchanges, issuer announcements, financial statements and investor relations.
Company exposure needs business evidence. Check actual release dates for Stock
Connect, margin finance, ETF subscriptions, trading disclosures and estimated
flows. Separate turnover, net subscriptions, holdings/valuation changes and
provider-estimated investor classifications.

## Fundamental transmission

Trace policy communication → formal measure → eligibility/implementation rules →
funding → execution/project delivery → orders/demand → collections/revenue/profit.
Identify verified links, beneficiaries, constraints and lag. Separate operating
demand/competition/margins from narrative attention. Distinguish banking liquidity,
actual credit expansion and household/corporate/government financing composition.
Monthly analysis deepens production, consumption, investment, property, trade,
prices, profits, credit and fiscal execution as relevant, with seasonality,
holiday shifts, base effects, nominal/real growth and cumulative-data definitions.

Read global developments through actual business exposures: Hong Kong/China ADRs,
renminbi, dollar, domestic/foreign yields, commodities and overseas companies.
Explain demand, costs, currency, discount rates and competition; distinguish direct
transmission, sentiment and structural business differences.

## Market pricing, supply, and cadence depth

Separate broad indices, majority stocks, operating themes and short-lived attention.
Use comparable samples, medians, breadth, size/styles, activity and internal sector
participation where available. Select sectors/themes after broad scanning with
direct-exposure representatives. Assess operating evidence, expectations priced and
catalysts awaiting delivery separately. Full-week breadth uses full-week stock returns.

Premarket emphasizes closure-period policy/news and new pricing questions.
Postmarket emphasizes chronology, index/stock divergence and separate post-close
news. Weekly tests durable operating changes and average daily activity for short
weeks. Monthly deepens industry demand/inventory/capex/competition, realized and
expected earnings, sector-appropriate valuation and financing/supply: IPOs,
refinancing, reductions, unlocks, dividends, executed buybacks, fund flows and margin
finance. Separate availability/authorization from transactions and actual selling.
Valuation specifies forecast horizon, cycle earnings, losses, asset quality,
metric/sample; exact decomposition requires compatible data.

## Risk and conclusion

Select disclosure, financing/dilution, unlock, governance/regulatory, liquidity,
suspension/resumption and product-rule risks with concrete relevance to the thesis.
Verify board/stock/ETF rules. Conclude with the main judgment, evidence needed next,
and principal reason to revise. Existing theses keep their evidence horizon.
