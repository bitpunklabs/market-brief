# Cadence requirements

Apply the selected cadence and market boundary conventions. These are distinct
research jobs; organization and section titles are design choices.

## Premarket — equities

- Default: next local trading session whose regular open is ahead. Verify holidays,
  closures, time zones, daylight saving and early closes. Early editions identify
  important intervals/events still uncovered. Historical dates use pre-open public facts.
- News: previous regular close through execution cutoff, entire weekend/holiday
  closure included. Maintain 5/20-trading-day context.
- Determine new expectation gaps, macro/cross-market transmission, company/sector
  implications, and questions for the target day. Compare the previous completed
  session. Distinguish futures/spot/regular close/extended/live quotes and baselines.
- Core conclusions; about 5–8 events; macro/cross-market; material earnings;
  about 6–8 sectors/themes; substantive outlook; today and next 5 trading days'
  events/risks; concise final priorities. Counts adjust to material evidence.
- Chinese report about 2,000–3,000 characters; core summary about 200.

## Postmarket — equities

- Default: most recent completed regular session. Separate price/news cutoffs and
  pre-open/intraday/post-close/older background information. Historical dates default
  to regular close; an explicit later cutoff permits a separate after-hours update.
- Explain repricing and reaction evolution. Compare indices, majority stocks,
  sectors/themes and 5/20-day context. Test leading attributions with chronology,
  persistence/reversal, and alternatives.
- Core conclusions; performance/participation; material events and price chronology;
  sectors/themes; macro cross-checks; real prior premarket review when available;
  post-close updates; next-session outlook and next 5 trading days' events/risks;
  final revisions. Carry unfinished longer theses forward.
- Separate results, guidance and preliminary after-hours information; conference-call
  status affects confidence. Chinese report about 2,000–3,000; summary about 200.

## Daily — continuous Crypto

- Default as-of T: actual research cutoff; review [T−24h,T], primary outlook next
  24h, additional 24–72h discussion, next 7 calendar days' catalysts. Include
  weekends/holidays. State measured endpoints and 7/30-day context.
- Historical date D without clock: UTC day [D 00:00,D+1 00:00), default cutoff
  at its end. Explicit as-of: previous 24h. Future dates receive an early edition
  based on available evidence and an identified incomplete retrospective interval.
- Separate pre-price-endpoint events, news between price endpoint and cutoff, and
  later measurements of older activity. Prior reports may overlap or leave gaps;
  evaluate actual post-publication windows.
- Core conclusions; performance/breadth; about 3–5 developments; spot/derivatives/
  supply/liquidity; macro cross-checks; about 3–5 themes; actual prior assessments;
  substantive outlook/events; risks/final priorities. Slow economics deepen when changed.
- Chinese report about 2,000–3,000 characters; summary about 200.

## Weekly

- Default: most recent completed calendar trading week with trading activity for
  equities; last completed Monday-to-Monday UTC week for Crypto. Explicit week-to-date
  requests identify incomplete comparison limitations.
- Equity returns: previous week's final regular close to reviewed week's final close.
  Crypto: matching Monday 00:00 UTC boundaries including weekends. Use 4/13-week
  context. State dates and actual trading/calendar days.
- Explain persistence, turning points, participation, rotation, operating evidence
  and change versus previous week. Normalize activity for short weeks; classify
  stocks by their full-week returns.
- Equity post-close news goes in weekend updates; Crypto post-Monday-endpoint news
  goes in post-week updates. Historical weeks use original cutoffs; separately
  requested current updates have their own boundary.
- Core conclusions; market/styles; expectation gaps (equities about 5–8, Crypto
  about 4–6); macro; company/protocol economics; sectors/themes (equities about 6–8,
  Crypto about 5–8); post-period updates; flows/positioning risks; substantive next-week
  and 2–4-week outlook/events; real prior report review; carry-forward judgments.
- Chinese report about 2,500–3,500 characters; summary about 250.

## Monthly

- Default: last completed calendar month locally; Crypto [first day 00:00 UTC,
  next month's first day 00:00 UTC). Explicit month-to-date identifies observed
  and remaining intervals.
- Equity returns: previous month's final close to reviewed month's final close;
  Crypto: matching UTC month boundary prices. Use 3/6-month context, 12 months when
  useful. State trading/calendar days. Compare flow totals and daily averages;
  TVL/supply/open interest/holdings retain stock meaning.
- Historical months use original month-end public information and vintages;
  current default editions may add bounded post-month updates. Distinguish equity
  post-last-close information from earlier price attribution.
- Operating delivery and valuation/embedded expectations are central, distinct
  responsibilities. Equities: demand, margins, cash flow, investment and credit.
  Crypto: sustained adoption, holder economics, dilution and liquidity. Compare
  beginning/end expectations on compatible horizons/samples, with revisions/lags.
- Core assessment; performance/styles; macro/policy; earnings or adoption cycle;
  valuation/expectations; sectors/themes (equities about 6–8, Crypto about 6–10);
  flows/supply; concentration/system risks; material events and post-month updates;
  next-month and 1–3-month outlook; real prior review; carry-forward judgment.
  Distinguish 1–2-week event effects from 1–3-month operating validation.
- Chinese report about 3,000–4,500 characters; summary about 300.
