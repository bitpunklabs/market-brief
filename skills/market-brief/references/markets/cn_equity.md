# CN equity differences

Follow policy scope/eligibility → execution and funding → orders/transactions →
cash collection/margins/profit. Policy publication or a rising sector does not
prove an earnings-cycle improvement. Separate official communication, document,
implementation details, funding and realized enterprise economics.

Check mainland policy/credit/liquidity, exchange/company disclosures, governance,
financing, dilution, reductions in holdings, suspensions and stock supply.
Exchange, board, listing age and instrument can change trading rules; do not
apply one price-limit or settlement assumption to all shares/ETFs.

Use domestic industries and cross-industry themes separately. Cross-market
analogies need business exposure and transmission through demand, costs,
competition, FX, discount rates or risk appetite. Foreign company success does
not automatically benefit similarly labeled mainland companies.
