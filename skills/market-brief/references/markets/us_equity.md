# US equity differences

Preserve an explicit earnings/guidance analysis in daily reports. Read issuer IR
and SEC materials for material facts; distinguish release from conference-call
information and preliminary from complete assessments. Match earnings bases
and compare guidance with sourced pre-release expectations when available.

Scan major GICS groups, subsectors and cross-sector themes, including weak and
defensive areas. Compare cap/equal weight, large/small, growth/value and disclosed
breadth. Explain concentrated index strength rather than inferring broad demand.

Verify regular/extended sessions, futures conventions, holidays, early closes
and DST with actual calendar inputs. Show important events in local market and
Beijing clocks. Fund/position disclosures may be stale; options activity alone
does not identify institutional direction, dealer gamma or forced buying.
