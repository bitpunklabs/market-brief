# Crypto market differences

Scan store-of-value/payment assets, execution networks, scaling, DeFi, stablecoin
infrastructure and other material functional groups before selecting themes.
Assess systemic exchange, bridge, custody, collateral and infrastructure risk.
Choose representatives by liquid exposure and active token economics, including
weakening or unconfirmed areas, rather than only the biggest daily gainers.

Protocol governance, status records, contract documentation and verifiable
transactions establish execution and exposure. Providers require clear methods;
network use, app revenue and token-holder economics stay separate. Access to
one venue or a few assets warrants a labeled sample, not a full-market claim.
