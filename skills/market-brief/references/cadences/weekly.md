# Weekly review

Use the latest complete market week, with 4/13-week background; analyze the whole
period directly, not a concatenation of daily reports. Crypto uses UTC Monday
00:00 through next Monday 00:00, end-exclusive, including weekends. Equity uses
the registered market-week definition and sourced actual sessions; the week is
complete at its final actual regular close, even before the weekend. Main news
and price boundaries extend from the previous week's last actual close to this
week's last close. A fully
closed week is not an observed trading week; short weeks need per-session flow
comparisons and whole-period constituent returns.

Explain turning points, driver persistence, participation, sector/theme
diffusion/rotation and constraints. Examine earnings/peers for equities;
spot flows, derivatives and protocol value capture separately for Crypto.
CN retains policy/cross-market checks; US scans GICS and disclosure lags.
Crypto focuses on roughly 4–6 events and 5–8 directions without quotas.

Separate explicitly requested weekend/period-after updates. Give next-week and
2–4-week events, hypotheses, observable triggers/invalidation and a concise
handoff of continuing priorities. Review actual old hypotheses by direction,
mechanism and timing; roughly three useful Crypto hypotheses are a focus target.
