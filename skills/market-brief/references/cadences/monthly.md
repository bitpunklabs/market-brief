# Monthly review

Use the latest complete local natural month, with 3/6-month background. Crypto
months are UTC and end-exclusive at the first instant of the next month.
Equity price endpoints use actual regular closes, separate from news cutoff.
Analyze period data directly; label any incomplete/early edition explicitly.

Evaluate macro/credit/policy transmission; operating or protocol economics;
earnings expectations and valuation separately; medium-term industry drivers;
capital/supply constraints and priorities. US retains same-horizon earnings
revision/sample quality, global capital and concentration. CN retains policy
execution and stock supply. Crypto retains adoption/token-holder value capture,
infrastructure risks, supply/valuation, roughly 6–10 directions and three
testable medium-term hypotheses without forced counts.

Use daily-average activity for comparable flow rates across unequal month lengths,
never for stocks such as TVL. Preserve performance, operating, constraint and
priority comparisons in readable short tables where useful. Separate allowed
period-after releases. Outlook: next month and 1–3 months, with test dates,
revision conditions and an actual old-report review only when available.
Default body: CN 3000–4500 characters; others 2000–2800 English words, provisional.
