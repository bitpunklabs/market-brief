# Equity postmarket

Default to the latest completed regular session. Separate regular-session news
and price attribution from explicitly allowed later releases. Match first-public
timestamps with event-window returns. Decompose US overnight gap and intraday
return by compounding, and compute contributions only with compatible weights.

Explain indices versus most constituents, decisive events/reactions, macro and
cross-market counterevidence, sectors versus themes, and any failed favorable
news response. US reports retain earnings/guidance and cap/equal-weight views.
Actual accessible premarket hypotheses retain their original conditions and
deadlines; no earlier report means no invented comparison or score.

Label after-hours disclosures and preliminary call information separately.
Provide next-session conditional scenarios, event risks and explicit judgment
revisions grounded in the original thesis. Default body: CN 2000–3000 characters;
US 1200–1800 English words, provisional.
