# Equity premarket

Default to the next session whose regular open has not occurred. An explicitly
requested trading day takes precedence; after its open, reconstruct what was
known before that open rather than switching to tomorrow. Include the entire
holiday/weekend since the preceding regular close. An early edition states
which relevant windows/events have not yet occurred.

Cover core conclusions; roughly 5–8 significant developments without padding;
macro/cross-market transmission; roughly 6–8 sector/theme/style directions;
today and the next 5 actual trading days' risks; and base/stronger/weaker paths.
For US include a separate earnings/guidance section and cap/equal-weight and
large/small comparisons. Use recent 5/20-session background for themes.

Each theme gets actual exposure, economic driver, recent state, counterevidence,
confirmation and invalidation. Premarket reactions are not regular-session
confirmation. CN checkpoints: after open, midday, before close. US checkpoints:
first 30–60 minutes, after decisive releases and late session. End with the
most useful judgment, observable signal group and overturning risk.
Default body: CN 2000–3000 characters; US 1200–1800 English words, provisional.
