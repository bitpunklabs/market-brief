# Continuous-market daily brief

Review the 24 hours ending at the actual cutoff, with 7/30-day background.
Historical date-only requests use that complete UTC day and D+1 00:00 as cutoff;
explicit timestamp requests use a rolling 24 hours. The outlook starts at cutoff
for the next 24 hours and a separate 24–72-hour horizon; add material 7-day events.

Deliver one integrated review/outlook: core conclusions; performance and a
disclosed breadth sample; roughly 3–5 important events; spot/leverage/supply;
matched-clock macro checks; roughly 3–5 meaningful themes; conditional scenarios
and calendar. Actual prior hypotheses can be reviewed before the outlook.
Explain trigger versus amplification and strongest competing attribution.

Treat the timestamp as a snapshot and retain actual data endpoints. Separate
later releases from earlier measured price reactions. Do not use BTC dominance
or a few high-beta rebounds alone to infer broad altcoin expansion. Default
English body target: 1200–1800 words, to be calibrated in real evaluations.
