# Equity research

Use actual exchange-calendar sessions including holidays and early closes.
Premarket includes the whole interval since the preceding regular close, even
across a long holiday. Postmarket separates regular-session attribution from
later disclosures. Distinguish regular close, pre/after-hours, futures and live
quotes; unfinished sessions are not completed returns.

Keep cap-weighted benchmarks, equal-weight measures, median constituent returns
and participation separate. Disclose sample, eligibility, weighting, missing
histories and point-in-time membership. A few leaders cannot establish broad
risk expansion. Weekly/monthly constituent statistics use whole-period returns,
not sums of daily advancing counts. Turnover is not net capital inflow.

Compare earnings on matched fiscal period, GAAP/non-GAAP basis, currency and
share count. Separate current results, prior guidance and new guidance; seek
peer/supply-chain corroboration. Study margins, orders, demand, cash generation
and capital expenditure when relevant. Buyback authorization is not execution.
Forecast growth from an NTM horizon rolling forward is not a same-horizon revision.

Align prices, currency, measurement windows and revisions. Compound overnight
gap and intraday returns. Index contribution requires actual weights and a
compatible method. Daily-average turnover can compare short weeks; stock
variables are not divided by session counts. Define price vs total returns.

Macro effects depend on growth, inflation, policy, earnings and risk premia;
falling yields or rate cuts do not have a fixed equity sign. Match cross-asset
clocks and explain stale/closed-market observations. VIX spot is not its futures;
bond ETF prices alone do not measure credit spreads. Options volume/OI does not
reveal net conviction or dealer positioning.
