# Crypto research

Use a continuous UTC snapshot, never a universal exchange close. Align venue,
instrument, quote currency and endpoints. Spot/index/mark/perpetual/futures/fund
prices differ; USD, USDT and USDC are not interchangeable during a depeg. Rolling
dashboard returns differ from complete UTC days. Overlapping 24-hour volumes
cannot be summed into weekly/monthly turnover.

BTC/ETH are benchmarks, not the whole market. Disclose a liquid risk-token sample,
excluding stablecoins, duplicate wrapped/bridged assets, leveraged tokens and
tokenized non-crypto assets from default breadth. Describe eligibility, missing
history, migrations and survivor bias. Define dominance denominators; supply
and membership changes affect market cap, which is not net flow or portfolio
return. Overlapping themes cannot be added as independent shares.

Separate spot participation, fund subscriptions, treasury purchases, exchange
transfers and stablecoin issuance. AUM/volume are not subscriptions, announced
purchases are not executions, minting is not deployment, deposits are not sales,
and withdrawals are not verified accumulation. Record publication lags and
wallet-label vintages; missing figures are not zero.

For OI/funding/basis/options/liquidations, preserve venue, contract, units,
aggregation, time and settlement conventions. USD OI can rise purely with price;
coin quantity and notional differ. Funding plus OI does not establish new net
longs. Normalize settled funding only on a stated settlement basis; predicted
rates are not settled payments. Liquidation notional is not cash loss and a
heatmap is an estimate, not observed future orders.

Trace usage → fees → provider-defined revenue → active distribution/burn/staking
mechanism → actual token-holder exposure. Company/app success or a proposal
alone does not establish token income. TVL is a stock, addresses are not people,
and token prices affect USD operating metrics. Compare flow rates by day where
appropriate, without dividing stock measures by elapsed days.

Separate circulating/total/free-float supply, emissions, vesting, unlocks,
restrictions, distribution, exchange transfer and actual selling. Assess supply
relative to credible liquidity, without double counting emissions/unlocks.
Verify incident status, loss estimates/recovery and unresolved exposure. Distinguish
policy proposal, filing, approval, effective rule and operational launch.

Traditional markets, funds and settlement channels retain their own calendars.
Label stale quotes and unreported flows. Verify UTC/Beijing event clocks and
local DST; block/epoch milestones may have estimated rather than exact times.
